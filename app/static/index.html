Google Looker Studio Connected to BigQuery

Data is transformed and available in Google BigQuery (Gold Layer).

  1. Go to Looker Studio and create a Blank Report.
  2. Select the connector Google BigQuery > My Projects > ferrostats > ferrostats_gold.
  3. Choose any of the 3 Data Marts: fct_ventas_diarias, mart_inventario_pareto_abc, or dim_productos.
  4. Click Share > Embed report > Enable embedding.
  5. Paste your embed URL by clicking the «Configure URL» button above.
